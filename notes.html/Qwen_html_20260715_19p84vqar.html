Rename Symbol eglot eglot-rename F2 — project-wide safe rename of a symbol and all its references.

eglot natively implements the LSP textDocument/rename and textDocument/prepareRename capabilities. It prompts for the new name, validates the scope via the language server, and applies AST-aware text edits across the entire project. True "safe" (scoping-aware) rename fundamentally requires semantic understanding, making eglot the optimal and only correct choice, as text-based alternatives (like wgrep or iedit) are inherently unsafe for project-wide refactoring. 		Working
